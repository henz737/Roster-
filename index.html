Malawi Airlines Roster Builder

Getting started

  1. Add the aircraft types you fly under Aircraft, with the cabin crew each one needs.
  2. Create each duty under Flights and routes: pick the aircraft, add legs with departure and arrival times, and choose the days it operates.
  3. Add your crew under Crew with their position, aircraft rating and base.
  4. Check the limits under Rules. The roster rebuilds itself after every change.
  5. Tap any day on the roster to add leave, simulator training, standby or a flight. Tap a flight to change its crew.

Print

In the print screen you can also choose Save as PDF. If printing is blocked where you're viewing the app, download the printable copy and print that.

Email the full roster

Send the whole roster to any email address, such as crew control or management. Separate several addresses with commas.

Import from another system

Bring in crew, flights, or leave, training and standby from a spreadsheet or another rostering system. Save or export it as CSV (comma, semicolon or tab separated), then choose the file or paste the text below. Column names are matched flexibly.

Backup

Save everything (aircraft, flights, crew, leave, training, standby, manual changes and rules) to one file. Restore it on another phone or after a reset.

Legality and fairness rules

Set these to match your authority's flight time limitations and your company agreement. The planner never breaks a rule to fill a seat; it leaves the seat as open time instead.
