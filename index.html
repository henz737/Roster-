Crew roster planner

Getting started

  1. Add the aircraft types you fly under Aircraft, with the cabin crew each one needs.
  2. Create each duty under Flights and routes: pick the aircraft, add legs with departure and arrival times, and choose the days it operates.
  3. Add your crew under Crew with their position, aircraft rating and base.
  4. Check the limits under Rules. The roster rebuilds itself after every change.

Flights and routes

Each flight is one duty day that starts and ends at the crew base. Times are local. Report and release times are worked out from the Rules tab.
